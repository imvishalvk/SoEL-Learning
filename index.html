What is the School of Experiential Learning (SoEL)?

SoEL is an innovative after-school program that blends global education models with AI-driven personalized learning. It focuses on 21st-century skills, creativity, critical thinking, and hands-on experiences through projects, internships, and real-world applications.

Who can join this program?

The program is open to students from all educational boards (CBSE, ICSE, IB, and State Boards) across India. It is ideal for learners who want to go beyond textbooks, explore future skills, and prepare for global opportunities.

What subjects and skills are covered?

The curriculum includes Core Subjects such as Global Citizenship, Sustainability, Self-Development, and Social Entrepreneurship — along with Electives like AI & Coding, Creative Writing, Digital Media Production, and Performing Arts.

What makes SoEL different from traditional tutoring?

Unlike conventional coaching, SoEL integrates experiential and applied learning through real-world projects, mentorship, and AI support. Students receive personalized academic guidance, career exposure, and the chance to participate in national and international competitions.
